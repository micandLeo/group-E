# Information Sheet
Project: Evaluation of XJTLU MSA Major Select Assistant
You are invited to take part in a user study for our HCI course coursework.

1. Purpose of the study
We want to explore user experience of this AI agent for XJTLU major consultation.
2. What you need to do
You will use the MSA chatbot to complete a task. You need to think aloud during the test.
After the task, you will finish a questionnaire and short interview.
Total time: ~20 minutes.
3. Risks & Benefits
There is no physical risk. Your feedback helps us improve the AI assistant design.
4. Data privacy
All collected data will be anonymized. We will NOT collect your name or student ID.
Data is only used for this HCI coursework and will be deleted after submission.
5. Your rights
Your participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without penalty.
You can refuse to answer any questions you do not want to respond to.

If you agree to join this study, please read and sign the consent form.
